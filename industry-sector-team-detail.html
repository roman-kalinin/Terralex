Overview

A global team for an industry without borders

The life sciences industry is at the forefront of technological and regulatory evolution, driven by rapid advancements in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, and medical device development. TerraLex's Life Sciences team provides comprehensive strategic legal advice on a diverse client base, including universities, governmental institutions, and companies at the cutting edge of the sciences.

Our global team addresses the unique challenges and opportunities of this dynamic sector — from first-in-human clinical trials and IP prosecution to commercial launch, post-market regulatory matters, distribution, and cross-border M&A.

Whether you are a multinational pharmaceutical company, an emerging biotech, a medical device manufacturer, or a digital health innovator, our specialists deliver coordinated counsel that reflects the latest developments and challenges in the sector.
