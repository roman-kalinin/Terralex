Overview

About 5A Law Firm LLP

5A Law Firm LLP is an Ethiopia premier law firm founded entirely by former Federal Supreme Court and Federal High Court judges. Our five founding partners — whose first names all begin with the letter "A," giving the firm its name — bring 114+ years of combined judicial and legal experience across Ethiopia's highest benches.

Headquartered in Addis Ababa, the diplomatic capital of Africa and seat of the African Union, the firm advises domestic and international clients on complex transactions, regulatory matters, and high-stakes disputes. We draw on a distinctive understanding of Ethiopian courts, regulatory institutions, procedural strategy, and dispute-resolution dynamics.

Our team comprises 22 lawyers and 8 support professionals, including 10 holders of LL.M. degrees and two PhD candidates, working in English, Amharic, French, and Arabic.

The firm acts for foreign investors and multinational corporations entering the Ethiopian market, international financial institutions, sovereign and state-owned clients, non-governmental and development organisations, and Ethiopian enterprises operating across borders. Our practice spans corporate and M&A, banking, finance and capital markets, investment, trade and regulatory affairs, litigation, arbitration and ADR, tax, energy, infrastructure and PPP, real estate and property law, ESG/NGO and development organisations.
