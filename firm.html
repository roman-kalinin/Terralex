Visit Website

About

TerraLex is represented in Cabo Verde by Abreu Advogados through its strategic local partner. This presence strengthens the firm's ability to advise clients across Portuguese-speaking markets and West Africa, combining international standards with local expertise.

The firm is active across Projects & Infrastructure and Energy & Natural Resources, with a strong pipeline in wind and solar, as well as in transport and social infrastructure. It is equally engaged in tourism and real estate development, along with the banking and financial services that support such sectors, as well as in fisheries, technology, and digital services — ensuring multidisciplinary support that integrates corporate, regulatory, and disputes capabilities.
