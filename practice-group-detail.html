Scope & Expertise

Where this group works

The TerraLex Antitrust & Competition Practice Group brings together specialist competition partners from member firms in the EU, UK, US, Latin America, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and Africa — coordinating multi-jurisdictional merger filings, leniency strategy, dawn-raid response, and behavioural-remedy negotiation.

Group members regularly act on global Phase II merger reviews, cross-border cartel investigations, Article 102 / Section 2 abuse of dominance cases, foreign direct investment screening, and competition-compliance programmes for multinational clients.

Industries served include technology and digital platforms, life sciences and pharmaceuticals, energy and infrastructure, financial services, automotive, and consumer goods. The group meets twice yearly in person and convenes a working session ahead of every ICN annual conference.
