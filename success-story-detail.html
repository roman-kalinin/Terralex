Success Story Cross-Border Referral

Real Estate Client Praises
TerraLex Responsiveness.

Americas North America Real Estate Litigation March 2026
48 hrs Referral to engagement
3 jurisdictions Coordinated counsel
1 client End-to-end continuity

A client in the real-estate sector approached the TerraLex team directly for an introduction to a US-based lawyer with significant litigation experience in their sector — a first in what would become a potential series of disputes.

01The challenge

The client's portfolio sat across three jurisdictions, and the upcoming matter would be the first of what looked likely to be a recurring series of disputes touching on commercial leases, cross-border title questions, and litigation strategy in the United States. They needed specialist counsel in their exact sub-sector — not a general commercial litigator — and they needed an introduction they could trust on the same call.

Just as importantly, the client wanted continuity: one TerraLex point of contact who could marshal the right local counsel as the matter developed across borders, rather than three separate firm relationships that didn't speak to each other.

02How TerraLex responded

Within hours of the initial inbound, TerraLex's regional team identified two member firms with the precise litigation track record the client needed and brokered an introduction the same day. The conversation between client and lead counsel happened inside 48 hours.

Where a typical referral might end at the introduction, TerraLex stayed in the loop — connecting the lead US firm with TerraLex member firms in Mexico and the Caribbean to pre-stage local-counsel arrangements for the wider portfolio, before the client had to ask. That coordination is what the network is built to deliver.

"TerraLex moved faster than any panel firm we'd dealt with — and the firm they put us in front of was already up to speed by the time we got on the call." — General Counsel, Real-Estate Client

03The outcome

The client was pleased not only with the speed of response but with the quality of the introduction itself: the recommended member-firm lawyer brought directly relevant sector experience, and the wider TerraLex network provided the cross-jurisdictional coverage the client needed for follow-on matters.

For TerraLex, the engagement is a textbook example of the network's referral promise: a single trusted contact, fast access to specialist counsel, and a coordinated handover that holds together across borders. The client has since returned for two further introductions.

  • One client, one TerraLex point of contact — across three jurisdictions.
  • Specialist sector counsel introduced inside 48 hours of inbound.
  • Pre-staged local counsel for follow-on matters before the client asked.
  • Quality of fit, not just speed — sector expertise was the brief.
