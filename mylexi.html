Frequently asked

Five things lawyers always want to know.

What is myLexi?
myLexi is TerraLex's in-network AI assistant. It answers questions about member firms, jurisdictions, practice areas and cross-border matters using the network's own indexed knowledge — so you get answers grounded in TerraLex content rather than the open web.
Who can use it?
Lawyers, marketing, business-development and knowledge-management professionals at TerraLex member firms. Access is gated by TLC Sign-On.
What languages does myLexi support?
100+ languages. Ask in your working language and myLexi will respond in kind — useful when you're briefing local counsel or drafting introductions for non-English-speaking clients.
How is my data handled?
Conversations are confidential and are not used to train third-party models. Prompts are scoped to your member-firm session and access logs follow TerraLex's existing privacy commitments.
How do I get access?
Sign in via TLC Sign-On. If your firm hasn't onboarded a primary administrator yet, contact TerraLex and we'll set you up.
