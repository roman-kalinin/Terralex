1) Hero: Fixed-background “global motion” parallax

What it looks like

Full-bleed cinematic city/airport footage or a stylized world texture.

Foreground typography is crisp and barely moves; background drifts slower.

Animation

Background: translateY at ~30–40% scroll speed (slow).

Foreground headline: subtle translateY (5–10%) + fade.

Small “data chips” (120+ countries, 23,000+ lawyers) drift in on stagger.

Why it works

Premium, calm, “global scale” instantly.

UX detail

Stop background movement after the first fold so it doesn’t feel gimmicky.

2) Section reveal: background-fixed “curtain” transition

What it looks like

Every new section feels like a new “room” you enter.

Background is fixed; content slides over it.

Animation

Section wrapper uses background-attachment: fixed (or fake it with pinned layers).

As you scroll into a section:

A dark overlay gradient eases from 60% → 10%

Content appears with a vertical mask (like it’s being “uncovered”)

Where

“Why TerraLex” / “Trust & Standards” sections

Pro move

Use a subtle noise/grain layer fixed on top for depth.

3) “Find Counsel” module: pinned search UI + map parallax

What it looks like

Left: a pinned filter/search panel.

Right: a map or abstract globe that slowly rotates/drifts.

Animation

Pin the left panel for ~1 viewport height.

While pinned:

Filter pills animate in sequence

Map slowly shifts (very subtle)

Search results count increments (count-up)

Where

Above the directory entry point to scream: “this is the product.”

Important

Keep it fast and usable; don’t over-animate inputs.

4) Micro-parallax: multi-layer “depth cards”

What it looks like

A grid of success stories (case studies) with layered imagery.

Animation

On scroll: cards move at different speeds (z-depth illusion).

On hover: card tilts 2–4° + shadow expands + CTA underline draws.

Where

“Global Collaboration in Action” (case stories)

Awwwards trick

Use a mask reveal (rounded rectangle) so imagery “slides behind” a frame.

5) Timeline “sticky chapter” scrollytelling (for Standards)

What it looks like

Left: big sticky headline (e.g., “Member Standards”)

Right: standards appear one-by-one as you scroll

Animation

Sticky left column stays fixed.

Right column:

Each standard fades + slides in

Progress indicator fills (vertical line grows)

Where

“How we guarantee quality” section

Feel

Calm, authoritative, enterprise trust.

6) Horizontal scroll moment: “Global Collaboration Stories” rail

This should be the hero interaction of the page.

What it looks like

A horizontal rail of story panels:

“Fastned EV expansion”

“IPO on ASX”

“Victorinox global compliance”

Each panel has: countries, practice tags, outcome.

Animation

Vertical scroll maps to horizontal movement (classic Awwwards).

While scrolling:

Background stays fixed

Panels slide horizontally

A world map line animates behind (connecting regions)

Where

Mid-page, after “Find Counsel” (so they believe the network works)

Pro details

Snap points for each story panel (feels intentional)

End with a final card: “Request an introduction”

7) Horizontal scroll alternative: “Cross-Border Guides” bookshelf

What it looks like

A “library” strip you scroll sideways:

Guides are like book spines or tiles.

Click opens a modal preview.

Animation

Horizontal drag + scroll wheel supported

Tiles have subtle parallax: cover image moves inside the frame on hover

Where

“Cross-Border Intelligence” section

Bonus

Add “Most used guides” filters that animate the shelf reordering.

8) Background-fixed section separators: “interstitial scenes”

What it looks like

Between major sections you insert a short cinematic interstitial:

airport runway

city aerial

boardroom / collaboration scene

Animation

Parallax zoom (scale 1.05 → 1.0) as it enters.

Text is minimal: one bold line, like a chapter title.

Where

Between: Hero → Find Counsel → Stories → Standards

This is how you get the “Awwwards pacing.”

9) “Metrics wall” count-up with scroll-triggered type morph

What it looks like

One big, minimal section with huge numbers.

Animation

Numbers count up (fast, 600–900ms)

The headline letter spacing tightens slightly as you scroll (micro “type morph”)

Background is fixed with a faint grid/map texture

Where

After Standards or before final CTA

10) Footer CTA: “magnetic” buttons + spotlight follow

What it looks like

Final CTA is clean, dark, bold.

Animation

Cursor spotlight follows slowly (subtle radial gradient)

Buttons have magnetic hover (they pull toward cursor)

CTA underline draws on hover

Where

Final conversion block
