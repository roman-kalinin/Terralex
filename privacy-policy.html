TerraLex, Inc. ("TerraLex," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit www.terralex.org (the "Site"), how we use and disclose that information, and the choices and rights you have with respect to your information.

What Is Personally Identifiable Information

Personally Identifiable Information ("PII") is information that can be used, either alone or in combination with other data, to identify, contact, or locate an individual. TerraLex collects PII only when you voluntarily provide it — for example, when you complete a contact form, request information about our member firms, register for an event, subscribe to our newsletter, or apply for membership.

Examples of the PII we may collect include:

  • Your full name and professional title
  • Your email address and telephone number
  • Your firm or organisation, postal address, and country
  • Information you choose to include in messages or enquiries you submit to us

Providing PII to TerraLex is always voluntary. You may use significant portions of the Site without providing any personal information. However, certain features — such as event registration, member-only resources, or direct enquiries — require PII in order to function.

What Is Non-Personally Identifiable Information

Non-Personally Identifiable Information ("non-PII") is information that, on its own, cannot be used to identify a specific individual. When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain non-PII through standard web technologies. This information helps us understand how visitors use the Site so that we can improve its content, performance, and accessibility.

Non-PII we may collect includes:

  • Your browser type, operating system, and device category
  • The referring URL or search engine that brought you to our Site
  • Pages viewed, time spent on each page, and click-through paths
  • Approximate geographic location derived from your IP address
  • Aggregated, anonymised statistics about Site traffic and engagement

Non-PII does not allow us to identify you personally and is used solely for analytics, security monitoring, and Site optimisation.

Use of Cookies

A "cookie" is a small text file that a website places on your device when you visit it. Cookies allow the Site to recognise your device on subsequent visits and to remember information about your session. TerraLex uses two categories of cookies on this Site: process cookies and session-state cookies.

Process Cookies

Process cookies support core functionality of the Site. They allow you to navigate between pages, complete forms, and access secure areas. Without these cookies, certain services cannot be provided. Process cookies do not gather information about you that could be used for marketing or remembering where you have been on the Internet.

Session-State Cookies

Session-state cookies collect information about how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are visited most often, whether visitors receive error messages from certain pages, and how they move through the Site. These cookies help us improve the way the Site works and tailor content to user needs. The information collected is aggregated and anonymous; it does not identify any individual visitor.

Most web browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you choose to disable cookies, some features of the Site may not function as intended.

Information Use

TerraLex uses the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, requests, and communications
  • To provide information about our member firms, events, publications, and services
  • To administer membership, registrations, and access to member-only resources
  • To send newsletters and other communications you have requested or subscribed to
  • To analyse, improve, and personalise the content and performance of the Site
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, security incidents, and abuse
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

European Economic Area and GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and equivalent local laws apply to our processing of your personal information. TerraLex acts as the data controller for personal information collected through this Site.

We rely on the following lawful bases to process your personal information:

  • Consent — where you have given us clear consent to process your personal information for a specific purpose, such as subscribing to a newsletter.
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to fulfil a contract we have with you, or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for our legitimate interests (or those of a third party), provided your rights and interests do not override those interests.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process your personal information to comply with the law.

Subject to applicable conditions and exemptions, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information; the right to data portability; and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided at the end of this policy.

Disclosure of Your Information

TerraLex does not sell your personal information. We may disclose your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Member firms — when you specifically request information about, or contact with, a TerraLex member firm, we may share your enquiry with that firm so they can respond directly.
  • Service providers — we share information with trusted vendors who perform services on our behalf (such as hosting, analytics, email delivery, and event management) under contracts that require them to safeguard your information.
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose information when required to do so by law, court order, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TerraLex, our members, or others.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, reorganisation, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

Data Security

TerraLex maintains commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted-access systems, and regular security review of our hosting infrastructure.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is entirely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. The retention period varies depending on the nature of the information and the purpose for which it was provided.

When personal information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised so that it can no longer be associated with you.

Data Transfer

TerraLex is headquartered in the United States, and our member firms operate in 134 countries. As a result, your personal information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

Where personal information is transferred outside the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place — including, where relevant, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses — to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

External Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, including the websites of our member firms. TerraLex is not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of such third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

TerraLex may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice on the Site.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically so that you are aware of how TerraLex protects your information.

Acceptance of this Privacy Policy and these Terms

By using this Site, you signify your acceptance of this Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Site. Your continued use of the Site following the posting of changes to this Privacy Policy will be deemed your acceptance of those changes.
