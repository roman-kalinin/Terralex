Our Mission

A strategic partner beyond conventional legal assistance.

For more than 35 years, TerraLex has connected leading independent law firms across continents to deliver innovative, forward-thinking solutions to the world's most complex legal challenges.

Our member firms share a single standard of excellence: deep local expertise, an open collaborative culture, and a commitment to integrity and client service. When a matter crosses borders, our network responds as one — pairing the right counsel in the right jurisdiction, fast.

Where conventional referral arrangements stop, TerraLex begins: shared knowledge, vetted standards, and decades of working relationships that turn 141 firms into a single team.

Member-Firm Standards

Vetted for responsiveness, integrity, innovation, and client service.

Membership in TerraLex is by invitation only and renewed by peer review. Every firm meets a single standard built around collaboration — from response time to cross-border experience.

  • 01

    Responsiveness

    Replies measured in hours, not days. Members commit to a 24-hour intake standard for cross-border referrals.

  • 02

    Integrity

    Independent peer review, conflicts management, and a shared code of professional conduct across every jurisdiction.

  • 03

    Innovation

    Practice groups share precedent, technology, and AI-assisted research — including myLexi, the network's collaborative knowledge engine.

  • 04

    Client Service

    Every member firm is rated on client outcomes and re-engagement. Excellence is the entry ticket — not the goal.

Governance

How the network is governed.

TerraLex is run by its members. Officers are elected from the global board for staggered two-year terms. Directors at Large represent every major region and rotate on a four-year cadence to keep leadership fresh and globally balanced.

  • Board of Officers. Sets strategic direction, appoints the CEO, and oversees standards and admissions.
  • Directors at Large. Regional partner-level representatives elected by the membership.
  • Standing Committees. Standards, Admissions, Practice Groups, Technology, and Finance — each chaired by a Director.
  • Directors Emeritus. Advisory council of past leaders; non-voting, retained for institutional memory.
